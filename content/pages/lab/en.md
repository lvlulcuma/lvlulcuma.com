A home lab is a small practice world on your own computer: virtual machines and containers that you build, break
and rebuild. Nothing in it belongs to anyone else, so you can attack it, infect it and take it apart without risk to
real systems or real people.

It's where security skills stick. Reading about an attack is one thing; running it, finding the traces it leaves and
writing the rule that catches it is another. It's also what you'll talk about in job interviews.

You don't need a server rack. A laptop with 16 GB of memory runs most of the ideas below, and every tool they use is
free. Start with [Get started](/en/lab/start/), then pick an idea.
