1. I test only systems I own or have written permission to test.
2. Scope is written down before work starts, including what is out of scope.
3. Findings go to the owner first. Nothing is published until it's fixed and the owner agrees.
4. I don't keep data I come across beyond what's needed to show the issue.
5. Write-ups on this site use lab targets or properly disclosed cases only.
