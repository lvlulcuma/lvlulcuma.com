title: "Stopping attackers from blinding EDR"
summary: "Attackers often switch off protection before running ransomware, for example with a vulnerable signed driver (BYOVD), as RansomHub-linked attackers tried with EDRKillShifter in 2024. Defenses: tamper protection, no local admin rights, Microsoft's vulnerable driver blocklist (default since the Windows 11 2022 update), alerts when a sensor goes quiet, and checking EDR coverage against the asset list."
