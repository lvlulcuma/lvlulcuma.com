title: "Privacy by design"
summary: "Building privacy into features, as GDPR Article 25 requires: collecting only the data a feature needs, privacy-friendly defaults, retention periods enforced in code, real deletion when a user asks (Article 17), pseudonymized data in test environments instead of production copies, and input for impact assessments (DPIAs). Breaking Article 25 can cost up to €10 million or 2% of global turnover."
