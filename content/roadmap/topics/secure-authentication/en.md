title: "Building login securely"
summary: "Getting sign-in right instead of inventing it: a tested library or identity provider, passwords hashed with Argon2id or bcrypt (never plain MD5 or SHA-256), NIST's rules (at least 15 characters without MFA, no forced complexity or expiry, checks against breached passwords), MFA and passkeys, rate limits, safe password reset, and session cookies marked Secure, HttpOnly and SameSite."
