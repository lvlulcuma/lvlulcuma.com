title: "How containers are isolated"
summary: "What really separates a container from its host: Linux namespaces and cgroups, dropped capabilities, seccomp and AppArmor or SELinux profiles, no privileged containers or host mounts, user namespaces (stable in Kubernetes 1.36, April 2026) so root inside isn't root outside, and sandboxes like gVisor or Kata for untrusted code. Three runc breakout flaws in November 2025 show why."
