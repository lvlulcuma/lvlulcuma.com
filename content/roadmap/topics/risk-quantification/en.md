title: "Putting a number on cyber risk"
summary: "Expressing risk in money instead of colors: Open FAIR splits risk into loss event frequency and loss magnitude (primary and secondary losses), each estimated with its uncertainty and combined in Monte Carlo simulations into a loss distribution. The Open Group's standards are the Risk Taxonomy (O-RT 3.0) and Risk Analysis (O-RA 2.0). Useful when leaders must weigh risks against costs."
