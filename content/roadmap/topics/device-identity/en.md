title: "Device identity and keys"
summary: "Giving every device its own identity, not shared secrets: keys generated or injected at the factory into a secure element or TPM, X.509 device certificates for the cloud (AWS IoT Core, Azure IoT Hub), Matter's per-device attestation certificate, rotation and revocation, and no hard-coded credentials. ETSI EN 303 645 requires passwords unique per device or set by the user (5.1-1)."
