title: "Running a data protection impact assessment"
summary: "Doing a DPIA properly: screening new projects, describing the processing, checking necessity and proportionality, rating risks to people, agreeing measures with the DPO, and consulting the authority if high risk remains (Article 36). The WP248 guidelines list nine criteria; processing that meets two usually needs a DPIA, and national authorities such as UODO publish their own lists."
