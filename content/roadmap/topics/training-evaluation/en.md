title: "Checking that training worked"
summary: "Evaluating at four levels, as in Donald Kirkpatrick's model: reaction (was it relevant), learning (pre- and post-tests, practical tasks graded with a rubric), behavior (do people use it at work, checked weeks later with their managers) and results (fewer incidents or faster response). Satisfaction scores alone say little."
