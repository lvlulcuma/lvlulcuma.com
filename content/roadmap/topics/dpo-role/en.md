title: "The data protection officer's role"
summary: "The GDPR requires a DPO in public bodies and where core activities involve, on a large scale, regular and systematic monitoring of people or special-category or criminal data (Article 37). The DPO advises, monitors compliance, gives advice on DPIAs and is the supervisory authority's contact point (Article 39); in Poland that's the IOD, notified to UODO."
