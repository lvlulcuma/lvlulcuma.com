title: "Securing Macs"
summary: "Managing Macs as carefully as Windows PCs: enrollment through Apple Business Manager into Jamf or Intune, FileVault with recovery keys stored centrally, Gatekeeper and notarization, OS updates enforced through MDM, approved system extensions and privacy permissions (PPPC) so EDR can run, and no local admin by default. Since macOS Sequoia (2024), users can no longer Control-click past Gatekeeper's warning."
