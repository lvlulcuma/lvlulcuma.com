title: "Designing for resilience"
summary: "Designing systems that keep working, or recover fast, when a part fails or is attacked: no single points of failure, redundancy across zones and regions, a small blast radius, isolated backups and mapped dependencies, as in NIST SP 800-160 Vol. 2 Rev. 1 (2021). In October 2025 one DNS fault in AWS's us-east-1 region took many apps offline for hours."
