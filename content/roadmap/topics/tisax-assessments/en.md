title: "TISAX for automotive suppliers"
summary: "The information security assessment carmakers ask suppliers for: a self-assessment against the VDA ISA catalogue published by ENX, then a remote (AL2) or on-site (AL3) assessment, with labels shared through the ENX portal and valid for three years. ISA 2027 replaces ISA 6.0 and is due to become mandatory for initial and renewal assessments from the start of 2027."
