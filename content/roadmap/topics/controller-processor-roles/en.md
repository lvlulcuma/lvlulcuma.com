title: "Controllers, processors and joint controllers"
summary: "Who is responsible for what: the controller decides why and how data is processed, a processor acts on its documented instructions under an Article 28 contract, joint controllers set out their duties in an Article 26 arrangement, and subprocessors need approval. The EDPB's Guidelines 07/2020 (final July 2021) explain how to tell the roles apart."
