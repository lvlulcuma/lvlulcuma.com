title: "Fuzzing"
summary: "Feeding programs huge numbers of mutated inputs until they crash: coverage-guided fuzzers such as AFL++ and libFuzzer, writing harnesses for the code that parses input, sanitizers that catch silent memory errors, building a good seed corpus, and sorting crashes into unique bugs. Google's OSS-Fuzz has helped fix over 11,000 vulnerabilities, and its AI-written harnesses found a bug OpenSSL likely had for two decades."
