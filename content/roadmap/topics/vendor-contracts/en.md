title: "Security in supplier contracts"
summary: "Getting security into the contract: a security schedule, a deadline for breach notice, audit rights, approval of subcontractors, where data is stored, service levels, and return or deletion of data at the end. Under GDPR Article 28, a processor contract must cover security measures, subprocessors only with written authorization, deletion or return of data, and audits including inspections."
