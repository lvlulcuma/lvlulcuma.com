summary: "Deciding which devices may join the network: 802.1X with EAP-TLS checked through RADIUS by a NAC platform such as Cisco ISE, Aruba ClearPass or FortiNAC, MAC Authentication Bypass only for printers and other devices that can't do 802.1X, VLANs assigned by role, WPA3-Enterprise Wi-Fi and a separate guest network. After BlastRADIUS (2024), require Message-Authenticator or move to RADIUS over TLS."
