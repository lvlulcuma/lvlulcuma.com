title: "Managing firewall rules"
summary: "Keeping rule sets tight as they grow: a ticket and business reason for every rule, no any-to-any rules, removing unused, shadowed and expired rules, filtering outbound traffic too, logging what's denied, and checking it all with tools like AlgoSec, Tufin or FireMon. PCI DSS asks for a review at least every six months (requirement 1.2.7); in 2023 CISA's red team found an agency's outbound traffic largely unrestricted."
