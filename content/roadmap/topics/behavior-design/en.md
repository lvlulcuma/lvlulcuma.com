title: "Designing for secure behavior"
summary: "Changing habits by changing the situation, not just the message: secure defaults, fewer steps, clear prompts at the moment of risk, showing what colleagues do, and well-timed reminders. The Behavioural Insights Team's EAST framework (2014) sums it up as making the behavior Easy, Attractive, Social and Timely, starting with the power of defaults."
