title: "Choosing samples to test"
summary: "Deciding what to test: the population, proof that its source report is complete and accurate, random picks, and sample sizes based on how often a control runs. The UK FRC's review of seven large firms (November 2023) found minimum sample sizes of 10 to 60 for controls run several times a day and 2 to 4 for monthly ones."
