title: "Backups that survive ransomware"
summary: "Designing backups attackers can't delete: the 3-2-1 rule with one copy offline or immutable, backup servers kept out of the main domain with their own MFA, encryption, and restore tests against agreed recovery times (RTO and RPO). In Sophos's 2024 survey attackers tried to compromise the backups of 94% of ransomware victims, and the median ransom demand more than doubled when they succeeded."
