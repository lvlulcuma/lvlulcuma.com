title: "Pseudonymization and anonymization"
summary: "Reducing how identifiable data is: tokens, keyed hashes, generalization and aggregation, keeping the re-identification key apart. Pseudonymized data stays personal data, though the EU Court (EDPS v SRB, September 2025) said it may not be for a recipient who can't re-identify anyone. The EDPB's draft anonymization guidelines (July 2026) test for record isolation, linkage and inference."
