title: "Mandatory KSC audits in Poland"
summary: "Poland's amended KSC act makes key entities pass a cybersecurity audit at least every three years, the first by 3 April 2028, by an accredited body, a sector CSIRT or at least two auditors with a certificate such as CISA, CISSP or ISO 27001 Lead Auditor (or three years' practice). The report goes to the authority within three working days."
