title: "Variant analysis"
summary: "Finding the same mistake elsewhere once one bug is known: asking why the bug existed, writing a query that describes the pattern (in CodeQL, Semgrep or a script over a disassembly), and checking whether the vendor's fix covers every path. It matters: over 40% of the 0-days Google saw exploited in 2022 were variants of bugs reported before."
