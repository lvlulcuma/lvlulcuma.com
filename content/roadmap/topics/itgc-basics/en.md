title: "IT general controls"
summary: "The controls around the systems that every application control depends on, usually grouped as access to programs and data, program changes, program development and computer operations (backups, jobs, incidents). If they fail, automated controls can't be trusted either. COSO's 2013 internal control framework has 17 principles, and principle 11 is about selecting and developing general controls over technology."
