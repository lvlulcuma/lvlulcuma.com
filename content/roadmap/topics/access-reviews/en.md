summary: "Asking managers and system owners to confirm who should keep access, then removing the rest: review campaigns in an IGA tool or Microsoft Entra, priority for privileged and sensitive systems, no rubber-stamping, and proof for auditors. DORA's ICT risk rules require access rights to be updated at least yearly, and every six months for systems supporting critical or important functions."
