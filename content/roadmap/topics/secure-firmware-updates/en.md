title: "Secure updates in the field"
summary: "Fixing devices after they ship without bricking them: signed images checked on the device, A/B partitions that fall back if an update fails, version counters against rollback, tools like Mender, RAUC, SWUpdate or MCUboot, and update servers built to survive compromise (TUF, Uptane). ETSI EN 303 645 requires a secure update mechanism on devices that aren't constrained (5.3-2)."
