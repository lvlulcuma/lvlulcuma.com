title: "Handling data subject requests"
summary: "Answering people who use their rights (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection): logging each request, checking identity in proportion to the data involved, finding the data across systems, and replying within one month, or three for complex cases with a reason. The EDPB's access guidelines (01/2022) say asking for an ID copy is generally not appropriate."
